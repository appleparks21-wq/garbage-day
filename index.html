    How to play
    1. 1
      Drag pieces onto your boardYou get 3 at a time. Place all 3 to get 3 more.
    2. 2
      Clear lines and build combosFill a row or column to clear it. Clear again within 3 moves to keep your combo going. Combos multiply your points.
    3. 3
      Powers fire by themselvesClears fill your power meter, and combos fill it faster. Every time it fills, the lit power goes off on its own, in this order: ❄️ Freeze (their pieces freeze for 5 seconds), 🌀 Scramble (their pieces turn big and ugly), then 💣 Bomb (saved for you, and if you get stuck it blows up a 3×3 spot so you can keep going).
    4. 4
      Win the roundMost points after 2:00 wins. If none of your pieces fit and you have no bomb saved, you lose right away.
    5. 5
      Earn rewardsEvery game gives coins, XP and a reward chest to open (wins get the best chest). Combos of x2 and up drop extra coins. Spend coins on block skins. Online wins also earn trophies and raise your rank.
